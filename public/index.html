Hardware Pinout & Wiring Diagram

HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor

  • VCC → ESP32 VIN / 5V
  • GND → ESP32 GND
  • TRIG → ESP32 GPIO 5
  • ECHO → ESP32 GPIO 18 (via Voltage Divider: 1kΩ + 2kΩ)

⚠️ HC-SR04 Echo output is 5V. ESP32 GPIOs tolerate max 3.3V. The voltage divider protects your ESP32!

Common Cathode RGB LED

  • RED Anode → GPIO 14 (via 220Ω/330Ω resistor)
  • GREEN Anode → GPIO 26 (via 220Ω/330Ω resistor)
  • BLUE Anode → GPIO 27 (via 220Ω/330Ω resistor)
  • Common Cathode (Long pin) → ESP32 GND

Active Buzzer & USB

  • Buzzer Positive (+) → GPIO 25
  • Buzzer Negative (-) → ESP32 GND
  • ESP32 Micro-USB / Type-C → Laptop USB Port (115200 Baud)
  • Webcam → Integrated Laptop Webcam or USB Webcam

Distance Threshold Logic Reference

Zone Distance Range RGB LED Buzzer Camera Action
Zone 1: Safe 0.0 to 50.0 cm OFF OFF No Capture
Zone 2: Normal > 50.0 to 55.0 cm GREEN OFF No Capture
Zone 3: Pothole Warning > 55.0 to 60.0 cm BLUE Intermittent Beep Auto Capture (at ≥ 56cm)
Zone 4: Critical Danger > 60.0 cm RED Continuous Sound Auto Capture (if not already captured)

🔄 Re-arm Rule: After capture at ≥ 56.0 cm, system only re-arms when distance drops back to ≤ 53.0 cm!
